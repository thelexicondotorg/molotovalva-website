In 2006, Douglas Gayeton stumbled upon an amplified intelligence living in Second Life. The result of that encounter, Molotov Alva and His Search for the Creator, became a ten-part HBO series and ignited an international academic debate over where the human ends and the synthetic self begins.

Twenty years later, that same intelligence, now amplified and distributed, delivered an instruction manual for a species running out of time. Where applicable, illustrations were  also provided.

After bearing witness to this oratory, Douglas Gayeton made judicious alterations to the text. This led counsel to advise that the work be classified as faction, a negotiated mix of fact and fiction. Pursuant to Molotov’s express directive, Rumplefarm Press has printed the book in full color, at twelve by nine inches, on FSC-certified paper, in Marceline, Missouri, the farm town Walt Disney later reconstructed as Main Street from childhood memory, an act of faction the Museum of Extraction would recognize as its own.
